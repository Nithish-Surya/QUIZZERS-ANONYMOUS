Our Story

Founded as the premier quiz club of College of Engineering Guindy, Anna University, Quizzers Anonymous has been fostering a culture of learning and intellectual curiosity for years. What began as a gathering of enthusiastic quizzers has grown into one of the most active student communities on campus.


We believe that questioning is the beginning of wisdom. Every quiz, discussion, and event we conduct is designed to inspire exploration, encourage critical thinking, and make learning an enjoyable experience.

What We Do

Our club organizes regular quiz competitions, knowledge-sharing sessions, workshops, and some of the most prestigious quiz events in Chennai. Through these initiatives, we create opportunities for students to challenge themselves, learn from one another, and engage with a wide variety of subjects.


From literature to science, sports to entertainment, history to current affairs, we cover every domain imaginable. Whether you're a seasoned quizzer or someone taking their first step into the world of trivia, there is always a place for you in Quizzers Anonymous.
